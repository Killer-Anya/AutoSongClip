# V2 曲库试运行与回退复盘 / V2 Catalog Trial and Rollback

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

2026 年 11 月 13 日，V2 的 100 条演唱记录短时替换了线上曲库。试用后，项目决定恢复 V1 的 235 条记录，停止批量切歌和自动发布。两次切换只更换曲库索引，生成的页面沿用同一模板。本文中的 V1、V2 仅指歌切流程。

### 实际查到了什么

维护者按 V2 有产出的 49 场回查曲库：V1 收录 136 条，V2 收录 100 条，少 36 条。这个子集排除了 V2 没有产出的场次，也没有逐首标注录播中应收的全部演唱。235 与 100 是整个曲库的两个快照，以上两组数字均不等于漏切率。

对照 [2023 年 11 月 26 日同一场录播的 V2 日志](../logs/stream_pipeline_sample.log#L16-L54)与[线上 V1 曲库](http://101.43.7.149:18081/web/)。V1 在这场有 8 条记录，涉及 6 个歌名。V2 样本运行记录了 16 个候选和 3 条新增记录。按场次和歌名比对，部分歌名对应不止一次演唱，无法逐条配对。

| 歌曲 | V1 收录 | V2 样本处理结果 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 《我怀念的》 | 278 秒 | 221 秒候选中，被程序标为说话的部分占 9%，超过当时的 8% 门槛，未发布。 |
| 《好想好想》 | 两条 143.8 秒记录 | 249 秒候选的首条歌词排在 63.6 秒，未发布。 |
| 《爱笑的眼睛》 | 236 秒 | 248 秒，完成打包。时长差说明两版取段不同，仅凭日志无法判断哪一版的听感或边界更好。 |

在维护者按场次和歌名配对的 21 个样本中，V2 平均多保留 23.6 秒。延长可能带来不同结果。《讨厌》的样本从 131.9 秒延到 284.2 秒，末条歌词也从 129.6 秒移到 276.2 秒，提示 V2 可能保留了 V1 缺失的后段。另一场的《爱笑的眼睛》在 V2 中首条歌词是 19.77 秒，末条距结尾 17.2 秒。时长和歌词时间来自索引与转写核对，仍需原音频听审来区分有效前奏、尾奏和多余内容。

### 为什么 V2 没达到替换要求

1. **同一次转写影响多个阶段。** V2 从转写的演唱标签生成候选、修订切点，再用转写识曲和排轴。误标的说话、漏转的歌声或较长间奏都可能影响后续判断。日志中的首句过晚说明歌词轴未通过门槛，单靠它无法区分真实前奏、切点偏早还是排轴错位。[候选分段](../lib/seg_regions.py) · [切点计算](../lib/cutwin.py)
2. **硬门槛减少了收录，指标还不能代替听审。** V2 会拦截不确定的识曲、低覆盖和低同步占比。歌词首尾规则也会把较长的无歌词段标为异常。历史批次前 38 场里，这些问题是主要拦截项；统计单位是拦截记录，一场可能产生多条。《寻人启事》的样本只匹配 11/27 行，即使命中行的中位误差为 0.005 秒也未通过。这个误差以 ASR 为参照，不能说明整首歌的实际同步精度。[质检样本](../logs/qc_rejects_sample.json) · [批次质量报告](指标与质量报告.md)
3. **发布前缺少按同一场次和演唱版本验收。** 服务可以加载页面、返回音频，自动质检也能筛出可疑项，但没有完成两版在相同演唱上的整曲听审，或验证 V2 替换后会丢掉哪些原有曲目。这些证据不足以证明 V2 的切点、排轴或音质整体更差，也不足以支持用 V2 覆盖原曲库。

### 留给后续类似项目的做法

先用相同录播和分 P 建对照清单，以录播标识、分 P 和演唱起止定位每个版本。分开统计候选漏检、识曲错误、切点问题、歌词偏差和重复覆盖。对说话比例或首尾规则触发的候选，抽听原录播和切片，区分真正的多余片段与合理前奏、间奏和尾奏。用独立人工时间戳评估排轴。发布时先保留线上曲库，把通过人工验收的改进作为增量验证。

项目已结束，线上保留 V1。V1 的已知取舍是部分片段的前奏或尾音略短。V2 在个别歌曲上延长了切片，完整性可能改善，仍需以原录播听审确认。[V1 技术方案](V1-技术方案.md) · [开发日志](开发日志.md#简体中文)

## English

On November 13, 2026, a V2 catalog of 100 performance entries briefly replaced the live library. After reviewing the trial, the project restored the 235-entry V1 catalog and stopped batch clipping and automated publication. Both catalog switches used the same player template and changed only the song index. V1 and V2 in this document refer only to the clipping workflow.

### What the evidence shows

The maintainer compared the 49 sessions with V2 output. V1 had 136 entries and V2 had 100, a decrease of 36. This subset excludes sessions with no V2 output and has no complete manual inventory of the performances that should have been captured. Neither these figures nor the full catalog counts measure the miss rate.

We compared the [November 26, 2023 recording's V2 run log](../logs/stream_pipeline_sample.log#L16-L54) with the [live V1 catalog](http://101.43.7.149:18081/web/). V1 lists eight entries across six titles for this recording. The logged V2 run found 16 candidate regions and added three entries. We matched recording and song title. Some titles appear more than once, so the evidence does not establish a one-to-one match of performances.

| Song | V1 catalog | V2 sample run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 我怀念的 | 278 seconds | The classifier labeled 9% of the 221-second candidate as speech, above the 8% limit used in the run. The candidate was not published. |
| 好想好想 | Two 143.8-second entries | The first timed lyric in the 249-second candidate was at 63.6 seconds. The candidate was not published. |
| 爱笑的眼睛 | 236 seconds | Packaged at 248 seconds. The duration difference shows different cut boundaries, but the log cannot establish which sounds better or captures the performance more completely. |

Across 21 samples the maintainer matched by session and title, V2 clips were 23.6 seconds longer on average. More audio can have different effects. A sample of 讨厌 went from 131.9 to 284.2 seconds, and its last timed lyric moved from 129.6 to 276.2 seconds, suggesting that V2 may have recovered part of the performance. For 爱笑的眼睛 from another recording, the first V2 lyric started at 19.77 seconds and the last was 17.2 seconds before the end. These are catalog and transcript checks. Listening to the source is needed to separate intended intros and endings from unwanted audio.

### Why V2 was not ready to replace V1

1. **Several stages depend on the same transcript.** V2 uses singing labels to generate candidates and refine boundaries, then uses the transcript for song identification and lyric alignment. Misclassified speech, missed vocals, or long instrumental passages can affect later stages. A late first lyric identifies a failed check, but does not by itself distinguish a real intro from a cut that starts too early or a lyric alignment error. [Candidate segmentation](../lib/seg_regions.py) · [Boundary refinement](../lib/cutwin.py)
2. **Hard gates reduced coverage, and the scores still needed listening review.** V2 blocks uncertain song matches and low lyric coverage or synchronization, and flags long spans without lyrics near clip boundaries. These were leading rejection categories in the historical batch's first 38 sessions. The counts are rejection records and can include multiple records per session. The sample for 寻人启事 matched only 11 of 27 lines despite a median error of 0.005 seconds on matched lines. The timing reference was automatic speech recognition (ASR), so the score does not establish synchronization across the performance. [QC examples](../logs/qc_rejects_sample.json) · [Batch quality report](指标与质量报告.md#english-summary)
3. **The release lacked matched listening checks.** The page could load and serve audio, and the gates could flag candidates for review. The trial did not complete end-to-end listening on the same performances or verify which existing catalog entries would disappear. The available evidence supports keeping V1 and does not establish that V2 was worse across all cut boundaries, lyric timings, or audio quality.

### What a similar project should do next time

Start with the same recordings and video parts, and identify each take by its source and time range. Count missed candidates, song identification errors, bad cuts, lyric timing errors, and overwritten takes separately. Listen to flagged clips against the source to distinguish unwanted speech or extra footage from valid intros, instrumentals, and outros. Measure timing against independently annotated lyrics. Keep the live catalog in place and validate reviewed improvements as additions before replacing it.

The project has concluded and V1 remains online. Its known tradeoff is that some clips have a shortened intro or ending. V2 made some cuts longer, which may preserve more of the performance and needs listening against the original to confirm. [V1 architecture](V1-技术方案.md#english) · [Development log](开发日志.md#english)
