# 质量要求 · Quality Requirements

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

### V1 听审要点

- 对照原录播抽听首尾，确认唱段连续，首句与最后一个尾音完整。少数 V1 片段前奏或尾音略短，是已知限制。
- 核对歌名和参考歌词是否对应同一演唱版本，抽查首句、副歌和末句的歌词同步，再确认网页可正常播放。
- 原始脚本检查歌词至少 5 行、覆盖率至少 80%、锚定率至少 85%，时间戳须严格递增。V1 也做人工抽听，未用 MiMo。下文的规范参考和其余量化门槛对应 **V2 实验**。

### V2 实验的测量参考

SongCut 参考广播音频的响度测量规范与专业字幕交付指南，再根据歌回录播的原声保留、长音与间奏调整验收方式。下表区分外部参考、项目阈值和人工验收。项目阈值适用于当前实现，外部规范的交付合规需要另行检验。

| 项目 | 行业参考 | SongCut 的应用 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 音频响度与真峰值 | ITU-R BS.1770-5 定义测量方法。EBU R 128 的广播节目目标响度为 −23 LUFS，线性制作音频的真峰值上限为 −1 dBTP。 | 用 FFmpeg 测量整段 LUFS 和 dBTP，保留原码流的响度。低于 −24 LUFS、高于 −6 LUFS 或真峰值高于 −1 dBTP 时仅记录告警。 |
| 音频切片与完整性 | FFmpeg 的 streamcopy 可直接复制压缩码流。源音轨的参数与项目保留前奏、尾音的目标共同决定验收。 | 源音轨须为 AAC、44.1 或 48 kHz、立体声。码率可读取时须 ≥128 kb/s。成品编码、采样率和声道须与源一致，实测时长与计划切片之差须 ≤0.15 s。 |
| 字幕可读性 | Netflix 通用规范给出字幕事件 5/6–7 s 的显示时长。简体中文指南的常规字幕上限为每行 16 字，成人节目每秒 9 字。 | ASS 生成器以每行 16 字作为尝试折行的阈值，并设置逐字高亮时长下限 80 ms。播放器显示逐行歌词，长音和间奏可以延长显示。代码目前没有强制检查每行字数或阅读速度。 |
| 歌词与演唱同步 | Netflix 时间指南建议字幕从音频起始帧开始，通常接受 1–2 帧内的起点偏差。24 fps 下约为 42–83 ms。该指南同时考虑字幕显示时长与镜头切换。 | 以相邻 LRC 行起始时间和自动转写的词级时间作可复查的信号。0.60 s 行起点间隔、0.30 s 中位误差和 0.90 s 第 95 百分位误差是项目内的歌词筛选值。它们与字幕帧级验收使用不同参照。 |

行业来源：[ITU-R BS.1770-5](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I) · [EBU R 128](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128) · [FFmpeg Streamcopy](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Streamcopy) · [Netflix 字幕通用规范](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements) · [Netflix 简体中文指南](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215986007-Chinese-Simplified-Timed-Text-Style-Guide) · [Netflix 字幕时间指南](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051554394-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-Subtitle-Timing-Guidelines)

### 自动检查与发布验收

| 阶段 | 当前检查 |
| --- | --- |
| 切点与说话 | 在线流程以演唱标签估计首尾，在短时能量中寻找 ≥500 ms 的静音区间。初始留白为前 3 s、后 4 s，找不到合适静音时可使用留白切点。成品须 ≥90 s。在线流程统计完全落在切片内的已标记说话句，超过切片时长 8% 时拒绝。 |
| 识曲 | 本地歌词库先按去重汉字的命中程度召回。联网候选至少 5 行，去重汉字召回 ≥40%，能够计算的动态时间规整（DTW）锚定率须 ≥60%。之后仍有候选一致性和得分检查。 |
| 歌词门槛 | 至少 5 行，相对参考歌词行数覆盖 ≥80%，DTW 锚定 ≥85%，去重汉字召回 ≥66%。至少 80% 的可统计行应能在转写附近匹配，命中行相对 ASR 的中位误差 ≤0.30 s，P95 ≤0.90 s。 |
| 歌词时间轴 | 行起点严格递增，相邻起点间隔 ≥0.60 s。首行须在 20 s 内，末行起点不得超出片段，末行至片尾的间隔须 ≤20 s。超长行间隔可由间奏造成。疑似额外行占比上限 5%。 |
| 成品发布 | 核对 M4A 可播放、LRC 对应正确演唱、ASS 文件可渲染，并抽听首句、重复副歌和末句。MiMo 音频复核在 V2 在线流程中用于抽听判断，服务无响应或返回不完整时应人工复核。当前发布命令不强制检查这三件套是否都已正确生成。 |

ASR 词级时间参与歌词排轴，也用于计算同步指标。对齐误差反映与同一份转写的吻合程度。抽检真实时间精度时，应对实际演唱人工打点并逐行比较。当前歌词检查以汉字和拼音为主，纯英文、纯假名和纯韩文歌词不适用这组门槛。样本数据和统计口径见 [质量报告](指标与质量报告.md)。

## English

### V1 listening checks

- Compare the clip's beginning and ending with the source. Check that the performance is continuous and preserves the first sung line and final decay. Some V1 clips have a shortened intro or ending.
- Confirm that the song title and reference lyrics match the performance. Spot-check the first line, chorus, and last line, then check playback on the web player.
- The original scripts check for at least five lyric lines, 80% coverage, 85% anchor coverage, and strictly increasing timestamps. V1 also used manual listening and did not use MiMo. The references and remaining thresholds below describe the **V2 experiment**.

### V2 measurement references

SongCut applies broadcast loudness measurement and professional subtitle guidance to the needs of singing livestream archives, including preservation of the source audio and support for sustained notes and instrumental breaks. The table distinguishes external guidance, project thresholds, and manual checks. The thresholds describe the current implementation. Conformance to an external delivery specification requires separate validation.

| Area | Industry reference | SongCut application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loudness and true peak | ITU-R BS.1770-5 defines the measurement method. EBU R 128 specifies a broadcast programme target of −23 LUFS and a −1 dBTP ceiling for linear production audio. | FFmpeg measures integrated LUFS and dBTP without normalizing the source stream. Measurements below −24 LUFS, above −6 LUFS, or above −1 dBTP are logged as warnings. |
| Audio cuts and integrity | FFmpeg streamcopy copies compressed streams directly. Source media properties and the need to preserve intros and endings guide acceptance. | Inputs must be stereo AAC at 44.1 or 48 kHz, with a bitrate of at least 128 kb/s when available. Output codec, sample rate, and channel count must match the input. Measured duration must be within 0.15 s of the intended cut. |
| Subtitle readability | Netflix's general guide specifies 5/6–7 s per subtitle event. Its Simplified Chinese guide lists up to 16 characters per line and 9 characters per second for regular adult subtitles. | The ASS generator attempts to split lines above 16 characters and sets an 80 ms minimum for character highlights. The line-based player can hold lyrics over sustained notes and breaks. The code does not enforce a strict line-length or reading-speed limit. |
| Lyric synchronization | Netflix timing guidance places subtitle entry at the onset of audio, generally allowing 1–2 frames of offset, or about 42–83 ms at 24 fps. Subtitle duration and shot changes also affect the timing. | SongCut evaluates adjacent LRC start times and their agreement with automatic word timestamps. Its 0.60 s spacing, 0.30 s median error, and 0.90 s 95th-percentile error are local screening thresholds, measured against a different reference. |

Industry references: [ITU-R BS.1770-5](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I) · [EBU R 128](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128) · [FFmpeg Streamcopy](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Streamcopy) · [Netflix General Requirements](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements) · [Netflix Simplified Chinese Guide](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215986007-Chinese-Simplified-Timed-Text-Style-Guide) · [Netflix Subtitle Timing Guidelines](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051554394-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-Subtitle-Timing-Guidelines)

### Automated checks and release acceptance

| Stage | Current checks |
| --- | --- |
| Cut boundaries and speech | The online workflow uses singing labels to estimate boundaries and short-time energy to find at least 500 ms of silence. Initial padding is 3 s before and 4 s after the detected singing. A cut can retain the padded boundary when suitable silence is unavailable. Outputs must be at least 90 s. The online workflow rejects clips where fully contained, speech-labeled segments exceed 8% of the cut duration. |
| Song identification | The local lyric corpus checks overlap between distinct Han characters. Network candidates need at least five lyric lines, 40% distinct-character recall, and 60% dynamic time warping (DTW) anchor coverage where calculable. Candidate agreement and score checks follow. |
| Lyric gates | At least five lines, 80% coverage relative to reference line count, 85% DTW anchor coverage, and 66% distinct-Han-character recall. At least 80% of eligible lines must match near the corresponding automatic transcription time. For matched lines, median error is at most 0.30 s and P95 at most 0.90 s. |
| Lyric timeline | Line starts must strictly increase with at least 0.60 s between adjacent starts. The first line starts within 20 s. The last starts within the cut and at most 20 s before the end. Instrumental breaks can account for long gaps. The share of suspected extra lines is limited to 5%. |
| Release acceptance | Check M4A playback, LRC alignment with the correct performance, and ASS rendering. Listen to the first line, repeated choruses, and ending. The V2 online workflow uses MiMo for audio spot checks. Review tracks manually when its response is unavailable or incomplete. The current publisher does not enforce complete generation of all three assets. |

Alignment and synchronization checks use the same automatic word timestamps. The reported errors measure agreement with that reference. To evaluate real timing accuracy, annotate the performance by ear and compare line by line. These checks primarily use Han characters and pinyin, so their thresholds do not cover lyrics written solely in English, kana, or Hangul. See the [quality report](指标与质量报告.md) for sample measurements and their definitions.
